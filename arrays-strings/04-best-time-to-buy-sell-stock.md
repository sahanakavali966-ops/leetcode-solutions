
## Problem: Best Time to Buy and Sell Stock (Easy)

**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/best-time-to-buy-and-sell-stock/

### Approach
I used a single-pass approach to find the
maximum profit. I tracked the minimum price
and calculated the profit for each day.
I updated the maximum profit whenever I found
a better value.

### Complexity
- Time: O(n)
- Space: O(1)

### Notes
I learned how to find the maximum profit by
buying at a low price and selling at a higher
price on a later day.
