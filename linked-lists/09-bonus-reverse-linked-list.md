
## Problem: Reverse Linked List (Easy)

**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/reverse-linked-list/

### Approach
I used three pointers: prev, current and next.
I changed the direction of each node's next pointer
to reverse the linked list. Finally, I returned
the new head of the reversed list.

### Complexity
- Time: O(n)
- Space: O(1)

### Notes
I learned how to reverse a singly linked list
using pointers without creating a new linked list.
